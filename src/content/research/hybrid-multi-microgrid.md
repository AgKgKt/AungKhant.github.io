## Why hybrid multi-microgrids

A microgrid groups local generation, storage and loads so that they can be operated as one controllable unit. Many newer sources and loads — photovoltaics, batteries, electric-vehicle chargers, LED lighting — are natively DC, while the existing distribution network and much installed equipment are AC. A hybrid AC/DC microgrid serves both without converting everything to one form.

Connecting several such microgrids together raises the central question of this research: how power should be exchanged between AC and DC sub-grids, and between neighbouring microgrids, in a way that is stable, efficient and controllable.
