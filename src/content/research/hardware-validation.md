## Simulation

The system and its controllers are first modelled in MATLAB/Simulink, where operating scenarios can be explored quickly and repeatably.

## Real-time and hardware-in-the-loop validation

Real-time simulation runs the model at the speed of the physical system. In hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing, real control hardware is connected to a simulated power stage, so that control behaviour can be checked under realistic timing before any power hardware is at risk.
