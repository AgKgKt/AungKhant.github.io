## Opening section

The main text is written in Markdown. A claim that depends on a source carries a footnote.[^1] Where the evidence is thin or the reading is contested, say so in the sentence itself rather than only in the notes.[^2]

## Evidence and interpretation

Keep what the sources say separate from what you conclude from them. A useful habit is to give the evidence first, then mark the interpretation with a phrase such as "this suggests" or "one reading is".

> Quotations from sources are set like this, with the source given in a footnote.

## Images

Every image needs accurate alternative text, a caption, the holding institution or source, and a rights or licence statement. Reconstructions and AI-generated illustrations must be labelled as such and are never presented as documentary evidence.

[^1]: Footnote giving the exact source and page for the claim.
[^2]: Footnote explaining the disagreement between sources.
